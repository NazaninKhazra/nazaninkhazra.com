Assistant Professor, Teaching Stream
Department of Economics
University of Toronto

I am an Assistant Professor, Teaching Stream, in the Department of Economics at the University of Toronto. My research interests include applied microeconomics, real estate, household finance, causal machine learning, and artificial intelligence. My novel courses are Big Data Tools for Economists and Causal Machine Learning (graduate); I also teach microeconomic theory.

I am affiliated with the School of Cities and direct the Khazra Research Lab (K.R.L.) for Applied Economics and Data Science. I also serve as an elected member of the Academic Board of the University of Toronto’s Governing Council (2025–2030).

Pronounced nāh-zah-NEEN.
