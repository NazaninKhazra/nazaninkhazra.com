Apply to Guest Lecture (PhD students)

PhD students can apply to teach a guest lecture or tutorial in one of my courses. See Teaching the Frontier.

Research Assistants (all students)

Undergraduate, MA, and PhD research assistants work in the Khazra Research Lab (K.R.L.) and present their findings at the lab's monthly seminars. Openings are posted on the lab website.

Data for Economists (undergraduates)

A searchable catalogue of 75 datasets for project-based economics, each with a suggested research question, drawn from Kaggle, Statistics Canada, US federal agencies, the OECD, and others. Built for ECO225 and open to anyone.
