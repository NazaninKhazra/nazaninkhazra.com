Khazra Research Lab

The Khazra Research Lab (K.R.L.) for Applied Economics and Data Science is a research lab at the University of Toronto where undergraduate, MA, and PhD students work as research assistants on applied projects and present their findings at the lab's monthly seminars. Current projects include homelessness in Toronto, using City of Toronto shelter data, and the pass-through of taxes on menstrual products, supported by a SSHRC Insight Development Grant (2026–2028).
